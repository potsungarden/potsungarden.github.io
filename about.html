About PotsunGarden

PotsunGarden makes printable guides for people who want to grow some of their own food in pots on a balcony, terrace or windowsill, and have never done it before.

What we make

  • Week 1, free: getting a balcony ready. 8 pages, A4 and US Letter.
  • Balcony in 4 Weeks, 2nd edition (€9): the full 36-page beginner's guide, with weekly lessons and checklists, 12 crop cards, a calendar by climate and troubleshooting.
  • Autumn & Winter Balcony Starter (€4): a short companion for the cool half of the year, with six easy crops and an October-to-February calendar.

All guides are digital downloads sold through our Payhip store. Nothing is shipped. Read them on a tablet or print them. Listed prices exclude VAT; any VAT is added at checkout. An Italian edition of Balcony in 4 Weeks (€9) is also available in the store.

How we write

  • Any climate. We use three broad climate types (mild, temperate and cold winters) and your own frost dates instead of month names from one country, with notes for the southern hemisphere.
  • Metric and imperial. Centimetres and inches, litres and US gallons, °C and °F.
  • Checked advice. Growing advice is checked against the Royal Horticultural Society (RHS) and university extension services such as Colorado State, Maryland and Washington State. Where sources disagree, we use the more conservative figure.
  • Honest timing. We tell you what a few pots can realistically give you, and when.
